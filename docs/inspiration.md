# 60 Four-Bar Chord Progressions for Piano Improv

Triads only. One chord per bar. Loop each one as long as you like.

## Soloing Over These

**Major keys:** play the major scale of the key name. C major over anything in C, D major over anything in D, and so on.

**Minor keys:** play the relative major scale — three semitones up from the minor root.

| Minor key | Use this scale |
|---|---|
| A minor | C major |
| D minor | F major |
| E minor | G major |
| B minor | D major |
| F♯ minor | A major |
| G minor | B♭ major |
| C minor | E♭ major |

**Exception:** when a *major* dominant chord appears in a minor progression (the E in Am, the A in Dm, the B in Em, the D in Gm), raise the seventh of the scale for that bar. G becomes G♯ over an E major in A minor. That one note is the whole "Spanish/dramatic" flavour.

**If you get lost:** land on the root note of whatever chord is under your hand. It always works.
